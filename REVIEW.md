## The 4-clients-a-day math

**The target:** 4 clients a day on 5 days = 20 sessions a week, about 87 a month. At 90 € that is 360 € a day and about 7,800 € a month.

**Open 90-minute slots in the online calendar** (checked 10 Nov 2026 around 12:30; Mon 16 not checked; Sun 15 and Sat 21 had no bookable slots):

| Day | Tue 10 | Wed 11 | Thu 12 | Fri 13 | Sat 14 | Tue 17 | Wed 18 | Thu 19 | Fri 20 | Sun 22 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Open slots | 2 | 6 | 5 | 2 | 2 | 4 | 6 | 4 | 2 | 3 |

Total: 36 open slots, 27 of them Tuesday to Thursday.

**Demand, not capacity, is the bottleneck.** Simply filling the slots you already offer would add 3 to 4 sessions per bookable day.

**Your published hours cap you below the target.** Google lists 30 hours a week. With 90-minute slots that fits 4 sessions on Tue, Wed and Thu and 2 on Mon, Sat and Sun: 18 a week. Choose one weekly grid of at least 20 slots and publish it everywhere, for example Mon to Thu at 14:00, 15:30, 17:00 and 18:30, plus Saturday at 10:00, 11:30, 13:00 and 14:30.

**How many clients that takes** depends on how often each one returns:

| A regular client comes every | Active clients needed for 20 sessions a week |
| --- | --- |
| 2 weeks | 40 |
| 3 weeks | 60 |
| 4 weeks | 80 |
| 6 weeks | 120 |

Frequency is the biggest lever: clients on a 5-session card every two weeks halve the number of people you need compared with monthly visits.

**New clients needed (assumption to track):** if about half of first-time clients become regulars and a regular stays about a year, you need about 10 first-timers a month to hold 60 regulars, and 15 to 20 a month for the next three months to build up.

## Wording and legal check

Three items carry a risk of a costly Abmahnung and are cheap to fix. I'm not a lawyer; have a lawyer or the GSD check the final wording.

1. **Health claims.** The homepage lists complaints that "can be treated" by shiatsu (back pain, headaches, insomnia, panic attacks), and the treatment page says symptoms can move into self-healing and trauma can be resolved. The GSD's own journal explains that once advertising suggests shiatsu goes beyond relaxation toward treating illness, the Heilmittelwerbegesetz applies ([GSD, Shiatsu Journal 74](https://www.shiatsu-gsd.de/wp-content/uploads/gsd-article-pdfs/geldrosen_reform_des_heilmittelwerbegesetzes_no_74.pdf)); it forbids misleading claims and promises of certain success ([Ärztekammer Nordrhein](https://www.aekno.de/fileadmin/user_upload/RheinischesAerzteblatt/Ausgaben/2022/2022.06.027.pdf)). Replace the complaints list with:

   > **Wann kommen Menschen zu mir?** Viele Klient:innen kommen, wenn sie sich gestresst, erschöpft oder verspannt fühlen, schlecht abschalten können oder sich eine bewusste Auszeit wünschen.
   >
   > Shiatsu ersetzt keine ärztliche oder psychotherapeutische Diagnose und Behandlung. Bei anhaltenden Beschwerden wenden Sie sich bitte an Ihre Ärztin oder Ihren Arzt.
2. **Google category "Naturheilpraktiker".** It suggests a Heilpraktiker licence. If you don't hold one, change it (see the Google section).
3. **The slogan "Berührung, die bewegt"** in your logo is the GSD's campaign motto. That fits if you are a GSD member; I found no GSD profile for you, so if you aren't, ask the GSD before using it.
