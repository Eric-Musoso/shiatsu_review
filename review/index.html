Summary

You already win the search for "Shiatsu Münster": #1 on Google Maps and #1 in the organic results, with 33 five-star reviews, more than the other shiatsu practices in the Maps results combined (6). The bottleneck is demand, not visibility or capacity: your own booking calendar showed 36 open 90-minute slots in the 10 bookable days I checked (10–22 Nov 2026).

To reach 4 clients a day you need three things: more people who don't yet search for "Shiatsu", a website that turns visitors into bookings faster, and clients who come back more often.

Top actions, in order of impact:

  1. Sell gift vouchers online before Christmas (six weeks away). Vouchers bought in December become first-time clients in January to March.
  2. Put the booking button, the price and "5,0 ★ aus 33 Google-Bewertungen" on the first screen of the homepage. Today the first screen shows only three photos.
  3. Make your business data consistent: one phone number and one set of opening hours. Google, the website and the booking calendar currently disagree.
  4. Add offers beyond the single 60-minute session: a first-visit package, a 5-session card, a shorter "Shiatsu kompakt", Kinder-Shiatsu.
  5. Complete the Google Business Profile: category, description, services with prices, booking link, regular posts.
  6. Reach people outside Google search: client referrals, health-practice partners, company shiatsu, and optionally Urban Sports Club for weekday gaps.
  7. Rewrite the health claims to stay within the Heilmittelwerbegesetz, and show your training credentials.

The 4-clients-a-day math

The target: 4 clients a day on 5 days = 20 sessions a week, about 87 a month. At 90 € that is 360 € a day and about 7,800 € a month.

Demand, not capacity, is the bottleneck. Simply filling the slots you already offer would add 3 to 4 sessions per bookable day.

Your published hours cap you below the target. Google lists 30 hours a week. With 90-minute slots that fits 4 sessions on Tue, Wed and Thu and 2 on Mon, Sat and Sun: 18 a week. Choose one weekly grid of at least 20 slots and publish it everywhere, for example Mon to Thu at 14:00, 15:30, 17:00 and 18:30, plus Saturday at 10:00, 11:30, 13:00 and 14:30.

How many clients that takes depends on how often each one returns:

Frequency is the biggest lever: clients on a 5-session card every two weeks halve the number of people you need compared with monthly visits.

New clients needed (assumption to track): if about half of first-time clients become regulars and a regular stays about a year, you need about 10 first-timers a month to hold 60 regulars, and 15 to 20 a month for the next three months to build up.

Wording and legal check

Three items carry a risk of a costly Abmahnung and are cheap to fix. I'm not a lawyer; have a lawyer or the GSD check the final wording.

  1. Health claims. The homepage lists complaints that "can be treated" by shiatsu (back pain, headaches, insomnia, panic attacks), and the treatment page says symptoms can move into self-healing and trauma can be resolved. The GSD's own journal explains that once advertising suggests shiatsu goes beyond relaxation toward treating illness, the Heilmittelwerbegesetz applies (GSD, Shiatsu Journal 74); it forbids misleading claims and promises of certain success (Ärztekammer Nordrhein). Replace the complaints list with:

    Wann kommen Menschen zu mir? Viele Klient:innen kommen, wenn sie sich gestresst, erschöpft oder verspannt fühlen, schlecht abschalten können oder sich eine bewusste Auszeit wünschen.

    Shiatsu ersetzt keine ärztliche oder psychotherapeutische Diagnose und Behandlung. Bei anhaltenden Beschwerden wenden Sie sich bitte an Ihre Ärztin oder Ihren Arzt.

  2. Google category "Naturheilpraktiker". It suggests a Heilpraktiker licence. If you don't hold one, change it (see the Google section).
  3. The slogan "Berührung, die bewegt" in your logo is the GSD's campaign motto. That fits if you are a GSD member; I found no GSD profile for you, so if you aren't, ask the GSD before using it.
